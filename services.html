Epoxy Flooring

Industrial epoxy flooring is a highly durable and long-lasting flooring solution for various industrial and commercial environments. It's made by combining epoxy resin with a hardener, creating a rigid plastic material that is strong, resistant to chemicals, and easy to clean. This type of flooring is ideal for heavy-duty areas with high traffic and the potential for spills.

Anti Skid Flooring

Anti-slip PU (Polyurethane) flooring is designed to provide enhanced safety in environments where slips and falls are a concern. It combines the inherent flexibility and durability of PU flooring with aggregates or textures that create a high-friction surface. This makes it an excellent choice for wet areas, industrial kitchens, ramps, and production facilities where spills or moisture are common, significantly reducing the risk of accidents.

Metallic Flooring

use metallic pigments mixed into clear epoxy to create a marbleized, three-dimensional look on concrete. The pigments are made from mica and iron oxide — they reflect light and swirl within the epoxy as you apply it, so no two floors look the same.

Self level Epoxy Flooring

Self-leveling flooring is a polymer-modified cement or resin mixture that spreads out evenly on a subfloor, flattening and smoothing it. Usually applied before installing hardwood, tile, or carpet, it resolves uneven dips and cracks. Some resin versions act as the final, durable surface for garages or commercial spaces.

Vinyl Flooring

 a synthetic, highly water-resistant floor covering made primarily from polyvinyl chloride (PVC) and plasticizers. It features a multi-layer design that realistically mimics natural materials like hardwood, ceramic, or stone. It is popular for its durability, affordability, and low maintenance.

3D Flooring

Stylish, immersive 3D flooring and wallpaper for stunning visual impact.

Carpet Flooring

a soft textile floor covering made of twisted fibers (such as nylon, polyester, wool, or polypropylene) attached to a backing. It is typically installed wall-to-wall or in modular tiles to provide warmth, noise reduction, and a comfortable underfoot surface in residential and commercial spaces.

PU Epoxy Flooring

Epoxy flooring is a rigid and high-strength system that bonds strongly with concrete, making it ideal for heavy-duty indoor environments. PU flooring, on the other hand, is flexible and elastic, designed to handle temperature changes and dynamic conditions without cracking

Terrazzo Epoxy Flooring

Terrazzo is a composite flooring system consisting of either a cement or resinous binder/matrix mixed with chips of marble, glass, and/or a variety of other aggregates. It's usually mixed on-site and troweled in place, then cured, ground, and polished to a smooth, hard finish. Terrazzo is one of the most durable flooring materials available today. Once sealed properly, the material is impervious to water and stain damage. It also does not chip away like other materials. With the increase in popularity of this material, architects turn to terrazzo.ble underfoot surface in residential and commercial spaces.

Gym Flooring

Gyms use different flooring materials depending on the workout zone. Commercial spaces primarily use rubber in rolls or heavy-duty tiles for weightlifting, foam or cork for yoga studios, and hardwood or vinyl for multipurpose aerobic rooms and basketball courts.

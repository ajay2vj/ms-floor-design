Welcome to MS FLOOR SOLUTION

With extensive industry experience and a commitment to quality, MS Floor Solution has emerged as a trusted name in epoxy flooring solutions in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We specialize in a wide array of epoxy applications including anti-static (ESD) epoxy flooring, anti-skid flooring, wall coating epoxy, waterbase epoxy, and coal tar epoxy flooring. Our expertise also extends to creative interior solutions like 3-D flooring, 3-D wallpaper, and Vinay flooring, gym flooring, carpet flooring, bringing both durability and aesthetic appeal to commercial, industrial, and residential spaces.
At MS Flooring Solution, our years of hands-on experience have enabled us to handle projects of all scales with precision and professionalism. We use high-grade materials, advanced techniques, and a skilled team to ensure every surface we treat is long-lasting, functional, and visually striking. Our reputation in Chennai and surrounding areas is built on timely delivery, customer-focused service, and unmatched workmanship, making us a preferred partner for epoxy flooring and decorative surface solutions across Tamil Nadu.

Our Vision

To be the most admired Indian floor covering company offering the widest range of products which meet the highest international standards and to make such products the ultimate standard in quality and workmanship leading to unbridled customer delight.

Our Mission

To attain and sustain leadership status in all our areas of operation through customer satisfaction built around trust and fairplay. To make the brand name MS FLOOR SOLUTION synonymous with floor coverings, globally. To not only save but also earn the nation foreign exchange through R&D and a commitment to reduce our countries dependence on importing such products by making our presence felt in the global market. To contribute to the conservation of nature by making recyclable products. To encourage and inspire employee participation in the company leading to mutual growth.
